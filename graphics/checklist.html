What’s Included in a Standard Clean

Kitchen

  • Counters and backsplash
  • Stovetop and microwave
  • Sink and faucet polished
  • Floors swept and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilet, tub and shower
  • Mirrors and glass
  • Vanity and fixtures
  • Floors sanitized

Living & Bedrooms

  • Dusting all surfaces
  • Carpets and rugs vacuumed
  • Beds made
  • Trash emptied
